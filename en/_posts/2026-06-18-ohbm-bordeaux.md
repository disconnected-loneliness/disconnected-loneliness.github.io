In June 2026 Daphne Boucherie presented a poster at the meeting of the Organization for Human Brain Mapping (OHBM) in Bordeaux.

The poster addressed whether loneliness can be predicted from functional connectivity: the degree to which activity in different brain regions is correlated. The analyses used public data from the Human Connectome Project.

An earlier analysis, with 88 young adults aged 22 to 25, showed no significant predictive relationship between functional connectivity and loneliness. For the poster, the analysis was extended to 264 participants, with both resting-state and task-based scans.

Both analyses were preregistered on OSF. The preregistrations are listed under [publications]({{ '/en/publications/' | relative_url }}).
