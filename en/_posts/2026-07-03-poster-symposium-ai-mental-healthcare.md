On 3 July 2026 the second symposium *AI and the Future of Mental Healthcare* took place in the TU Delft Aula, organised by the TU Delft [Special Interest Group Mental Health and Tech](https://sigmh.tbm.tudelft.nl/). The symposium brought together researchers from AI, clinical psychology, and ethics to discuss the role of AI in mental healthcare.

In the poster session, Jenna Pfeifer presented the [synthetic photo-elicitation workshop method]({{ '/en/news/2026/workshops-picturing-loneliness/' | relative_url }}).
