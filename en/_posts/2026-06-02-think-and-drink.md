On 2 June 2026 Bianca Sutcliffe and Jenna Pfeifer were guests at Think & Drink, organised by Studium Generale in the TU Delft Library. The evening was about the connections people form with AI chatbots: from help with studying to conversations about feelings.

Bianca and Jenna explained what is known about whether people can form a human-like connection with a chatbot, and discussed it with the audience.

More about the evening is on the [Studium Generale website](https://www.tudelft.nl/evenementen/2026/sg/06-jun/ai-and-emotional-connection).
