On 2 June 2026 Bianca Sutcliffe and Jenna Pfeifer spoke at Think & Drink, a Studium Generale event in the TU Delft Library, about emotional connection with AI chatbots. With the audience they discussed whether people can form a human-like connection with a chatbot.

More about the evening is on the [Studium Generale website](https://www.tudelft.nl/evenementen/2026/sg/06-jun/ai-and-emotional-connection).
