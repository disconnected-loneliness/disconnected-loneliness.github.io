Disconnected started on 15 March 2024. In the project, TU Delft and Amsterdam UMC study, together with partners from practice, what technology means for loneliness in young adults.

Disconnected runs until 2029 and is part of the Loneliness programme of the Dutch Research Agenda (NWA), funded by NWO. Three early-career researchers will work on the project, in Delft and in Amsterdam.
