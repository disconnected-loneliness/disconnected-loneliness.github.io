Disconnected started on 15 March 2024. The project studies loneliness among young adults aged 18 to 26 and the role of technology, which can both increase and reduce feelings of loneliness.

The project is carried out by TU Delft and Amsterdam UMC, with partners from practice. It runs until 2029 and is funded by NWO through the Loneliness programme of the Dutch Research Agenda (NWA).
