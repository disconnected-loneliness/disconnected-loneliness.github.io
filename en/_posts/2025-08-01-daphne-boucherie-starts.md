On 1 August 2025 Daphne Boucherie started as a postdoctoral researcher at the Department of Radiology and Nuclear Medicine of Amsterdam UMC. She works on the neuroimaging part of Disconnected, on the relation between loneliness and brain function.

Three researchers now work on the project: Bianca Sutcliffe and Jenna Pfeifer at TU Delft, and Daphne Boucherie at Amsterdam UMC. See the [team]({{ '/en/team/' | relative_url }}).
