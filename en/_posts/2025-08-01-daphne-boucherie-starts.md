On 1 August 2025 Daphne Boucherie started as a postdoctoral researcher at Amsterdam UMC. She studies whether loneliness can be predicted from the way brain regions communicate with each other, and is preparing a study that follows young adults for a year.

Three researchers now work on Disconnected: Bianca Sutcliffe and Jenna Pfeifer in Delft, and Daphne Boucherie in Amsterdam. See the [team]({{ '/en/team/' | relative_url }}).
