On 19 June 2025 the team was at TEKNOWLOGY, NWO's innovation festival at DeFabrique in Utrecht. In a live demonstration, a camera filmed the area around the stand and an AI model described what it saw, including the atmosphere and the emotions of the people in view.

The demo was accompanied by a [poster](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/393087811_AI_for_Contextual_Understanding_From_Driving_Risk_to_Social_Dynamics) on the agreement between AI and people in judging the emotional tone of images.
