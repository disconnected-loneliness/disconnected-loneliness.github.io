On 19 June 2025 the team was at TEKNOWLOGY, NWO's innovation festival at DeFabrique in Utrecht. Visitors saw a live demonstration: a camera filmed the area around the stand, and an AI model described directly what it saw, including the atmosphere and the emotions of the people in view.

The demo came with a [poster](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/393087811_AI_for_Contextual_Understanding_From_Driving_Risk_to_Social_Dynamics) on how closely AI and people agree when they judge the emotional tone of images.
