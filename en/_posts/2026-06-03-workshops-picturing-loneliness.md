Jenna Pfeifer developed a workshop method called synthetic photo-elicitation. Participants write a prompt for an AI image generator to depict "a young adult who might be lonely", and can revise the prompt until the image fits what they have in mind. The submitted images are shown in a shared gallery and serve as a starting point for group discussion. The method builds on photo-elicitation, in which photographs support conversation about topics that are hard to discuss.

Three workshops were held between February and June 2026, at TU Delft, at Fontys in Eindhoven, and at Studium Generale in Delft, with 19 participants in total. Together they submitted 53 images.

Participants mostly depicted loneliness as social disconnection rather than physical isolation, for example a person in a crowd or in a group without interacting with the others. The images generally kept the scenario of the prompt, but often added cues that participants had not asked for, such as an averted gaze, a withdrawn posture, or grey weather. The groups discussed these differences between prompt and image.

The workshop code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/jennapfeifer/loneliness-workshop).
