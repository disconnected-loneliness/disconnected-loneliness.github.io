The first article from Disconnected, with Jenna Pfeifer as first author, has been published in *Personality and Individual Differences*. The study examined whether loneliness is related to attention to images of social rejection or exclusion. A common hypothesis is that loneliness goes together with heightened vigilance for social threat.

In the study, 241 young adults viewed four images at a time while an eye tracker recorded their eye movements. The images were generated with AI and were social or non-social, and positive or negative.

No significant association was found between loneliness and attention to socially threatening images. This suggests that heightened vigilance for social threat may not be a feature of loneliness in young adults without clinical complaints. The authors recommend further research with very lonely individuals.

Read the [article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.paid.2025.113415). The data and analysis code are on [4TU.ResearchData](https://doi.org/10.4121/b02ace68-8e95-4355-bcea-f073c52e8a14).
