In June and July 2026 the team held expert sessions with three partners: 8D in Leeuwarden (11 June), NVO in Utrecht (30 June), and NewHealth in Utrecht (21 July). In each session, staff of the partner took part in a shortened version of the [workshop]({{ '/en/news/2026/workshops-picturing-loneliness/' | relative_url }}) and then discussed possible uses, limitations, and risks for their own work.

The partners mentioned uses with clients who find it hard to put feelings into words, in psychoeducation, and in exploring a current and a desired situation together. They noted that talking about a fictional person makes it easier to open up, although the exercise can still bring up emotions.
