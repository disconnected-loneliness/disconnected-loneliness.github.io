On 28 August 2026 Baraah Baryhe graduated from the MSc Biomedical Engineering at TU Delft, with a study within Disconnected.

Baraah studied where people look when they judge how lonely someone in a photo is. In her study, 54 participants rated 102 AI-generated images of six everyday situations, while an eye tracker recorded their eye movements. Between the variants of an image, one thing differed each time, such as the facial expression, the weather, the objects in view, or the presence of other people.

Facial expression had the largest effect on the judgement. Objects and a cluttered background drew the gaze away from the face, but did not change the judgement of loneliness.

The thesis is in the [TU Delft repository](https://resolver.tudelft.nl/uuid:11cec5f1-aa32-44dd-a968-9be4e0083f8b).
