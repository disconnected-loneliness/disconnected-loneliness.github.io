On 13 November 2024 Jenna Pfeifer and Dimitra Dodou presented the project at the [annual conference of the LDE Centre Governance of Migration and Diversity](https://tudelft.nl/en/events/2024/bk/lde-conference-governance-of-migration-and-diversity) in Leiden. LDE stands for Leiden-Delft-Erasmus, the alliance of the universities of Leiden, Delft, and Rotterdam.

The presentation was about what technology does to loneliness in young adults, from social media to conversations with AI chatbots, and about the design of the project. Jenna Pfeifer also outlined her plans to use eye tracking to study whether loneliness is related to attention to signs of social threat.
